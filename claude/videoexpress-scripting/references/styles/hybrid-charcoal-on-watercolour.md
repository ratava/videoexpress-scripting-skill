# Hybrid: Charcoal Character on Watercolour Background

**Type**: custom hybrid (two media), tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "A mixed-media illustration, widescreen 16:9, in two distinct media on textured cold-pressed watercolour paper. The character is drawn only in deep velvety compressed charcoal with white chalk highlights, soft smudged shading and a few sharp contour lines. The background is painted only in loose transparent watercolour washes with soft wet-into-wet edges and granulation, and no charcoal in it." Video anchor: "Mixed-media animation: a charcoal and chalk character whose marks slightly re-draw frame to frame in a gentle stepped cadence, over a still loose watercolour background that stays settled, drawn and painted, not rendered."
- **Character design**: a dense dark charcoal mass with smudged edges, lifted lighter underneath, chalk highlights along the lit edges, one sharp dark eye; "no wash touches him".
- **Materials / rendering**: the darkest medium over the most translucent, so the layer contrast is the strongest of the five; the character's reflection may be painted as a blurred darker wash in the water layer.
- **Light**: a dawn or dusk sky wash whose colour reflects in the water; chalk highlights on the character's lit edges.
- **Motion**: head lowering and lifting, a ring of wash spreading, mist drifting as a soft wash; camera locked.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly two media: the [character] in charcoal and chalk only, the [background elements] in watercolour wash only; charcoal marks do not re-smudge or bleed, washes remain settled and do not boil or crawl, paper texture visible throughout, consistent character drawing."
- **Guards**: no wash on the character, no charcoal in the background, no ink, no pencil, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no realistic fur, no live-action look.
- **Known failures**: none in the test take (stag drinking at a misty lake at dawn).
