# Graffiti (aerosol spray-paint toon, street mural)

**Type**: custom, untested (first test: a caricature character beside a charcoal kettle grill)

- **Medium phrase**: "Graffiti street-art illustration in aerosol spray paint on a wall, widescreen 16:9, the painted image filling the whole frame." Video anchor: "Graffiti spray-paint cartoon animation in aerosol paint on a wall: thick black outlines with a bright white keyline, saturated flat fills with soft airbrushed gradients, fuzzy overspray halos around every shape, paint drips running down from lower edges, the wall texture showing through the paint, strokes and drips that hold their place while the character moves in a stepped painted cadence, sprayed, not rendered."
- **Character design**: cartoon caricature proportions: an oversized head, the defining features exaggerated (head shape, glasses, beard, grin), chunky hands, a slightly squat body. Every shape closed by a thick black outline and a thin white keyline outside it; a hard-edged highlight crescent on shiny surfaces; eyes as bold shapes with one white glint.
- **Materials / rendering**: aerosol paint only: flat saturated fills, airbrushed gradients inside shapes, soft fuzzy overspray beyond the outline, a few runs and drips from the lowest edges, fine spatter specks, wall texture (brick mortar lines or corrugated ridges and rust) visible through thinner paint. Décor is abstract only: arrows, stars, crowns, bubbles, splats, highlight sparkles.
- **Light**: a bright key read as a hard white highlight shape and a single flat shadow tone; a coloured rim of overspray glow (orange, magenta or cyan) behind the character to separate it from the wall.
- **Motion**: one bold beat per segment (a lid lift, a grin, a tool raised) then a hold; stepped painted cadence; locked camera or one slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly two-dimensional aerosol spray-paint graffiti on a wall, thick black outlines with a white keyline, flat saturated fills with airbrushed gradients, overspray halos and paint drips, wall texture showing through, stepped painted cadence, outlines and drips that stay in place and do not boil, swim or re-spray, stable colour, consistent caricature of the character throughout."
- **Guards**: no letters, no words, no tags, no lettering, no signatures, no numbers, no logos, no wordmarks, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no realistic skin or fabric textures, no live-action look, no realistic lighting, no clean vector look.
- **Known failures**: sprayed décor on a set wall ("graffiti flame shapes, stars and splatter") produced tag-like pseudo-lettering in a start image despite "no letters, no tags" guards; leave sprayed décor off backgrounds and let the medium carry the look. With a stylised reference in Consistent Character slot 1, the reference's own finish outweighed the medium wording (a clean cartoon look instead of spray texture). The style itself held from text alone in every take of the first test.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
