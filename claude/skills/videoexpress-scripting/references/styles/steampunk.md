# Steampunk (painted Victorian-industrial adventure illustration)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Richly painted steampunk adventure illustration, a Victorian-industrial fantasy painted in opaque oils and gouache with visible brushwork, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Painted steampunk illustration animation: a Victorian-industrial fantasy world of brass, copper, iron and leather, painted in opaque oils and gouache with visible brushwork and soft painted edges, warm gaslight glow, wisps of pale painted steam, painted, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: illustrated, slightly heroic proportions with readable faces; Victorian dress built from layered, practical pieces: waistcoats, long coats, corsets, high collars, leather aprons, fingerless gloves, brass goggles worn up on the forehead or hat brim, pocket watches on chains, tool belts. Name each piece's colour and material; keep goggles in the same place in every prompt.
- **Materials / rendering**: polished and tarnished brass, warm copper with green verdigris at the seams, riveted dark iron plates, oiled brown leather with stitching, dark polished wood, glass dials and gauges with blank faces, exposed gears, springs, pipes and valves. Metals told by painted highlights and warm reflections, not chrome-like mirror shine. Steam as soft, pale painted wisps that drift slowly.
- **Light**: warm amber gaslight or firelight as the key, with a cooler grey-blue daylight fill from a high window; glints on brass edges; gentle haze in the air. Keep the subject's face lit by the warm key.
- **Motion**: one mechanical beat per segment (a gear turns a quarter turn, a valve releases one puff of steam, a lever pulls down, goggles lower over the eyes), then a settle; measured, deliberate cadence; locked camera or one slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly painted steampunk illustration in opaque oils and gouache, visible brushwork, warm brass, copper and leather tones, painted highlights on metal, gears and fittings keep their shape and position, steam stays soft and painted, stable colour and painting of the subject throughout, no conversion to 3D or photography."
- **Guards**: no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no CG chrome or plastic shine, no live-action look, no modern technology (no screens, no plastic, no electric lights beyond gas lamps or glowing filaments), no neon, no legible text or numerals on dials, signs or labels, no watermark. Sound: name only transient mechanical sounds (a clank, a ratchet click, a single short puff of steam); a continuous hiss, hum or engine drone renders as background noise for the whole clip.
- **Known failures**: none in three test takes (an inventor turning a key on a clockwork device, a puff of steam, with a STEAMPUNK nameplate held from the start frame). Risks still worth watching: drift toward glossy 3D or a live-action costume film, gears changing tooth count, dial faces sprouting numerals, and steam sound turning into a constant hiss.
