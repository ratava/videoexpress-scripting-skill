# Flat Vector Explainer (clean modern 2D vector art for explainer and training videos)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Flat vector explainer illustration, clean modern digital vector art in solid flat colours on a plain white background, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Flat vector explainer animation: clean modern 2D vector art, solid flat colour fills with no gradients, shading or texture, a simple rounded character, thin dark line-art details on a plain white background, smooth simple motion, drawn as flat vector graphics, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: simple rounded shapes; an oval head with two small dot eyes, a short curved smile and at most a tiny line for the nose; hair as one solid flat shape; a simplified, slightly elongated body; simple mitten-like hands; no facial shading, no lashes, no skin detail.
- **Materials / rendering**: solid flat fills from a small palette (for example lilac, mustard yellow, navy and white, plus one accent); background props drawn only as thin dark line art (picture frames, windows, a city skyline, shelves); foreground props as solid colour shapes (a laptop, a plant with flat leaves in two colours); a plain white or very pale background with lots of empty space. No gradients, no texture, no drop shadows.
- **Light**: none modelled; even and flat. At most one darker flat shape under an object, never a soft shadow.
- **Motion**: one simple gesture per segment (a wave, lifting a mug, pointing at something, a step to one side), smooth and even, then a hold; locked camera or one slow push; background line art stays still.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly flat vector explainer art, solid flat colour fills, no gradients, no shading, no texture, thin crisp line-art details, plain white background, shapes and lines stay crisp and do not wobble or redraw, stable colours and character design throughout."
- **Guards**: no gradients, no shading, no soft shadows, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no realistic faces or skin, no paper or canvas texture, no brush strokes, no outlines on the character's face beyond the dot eyes and smile, no text, letters or numbers, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Flat Illustration Style. Artistly's 2d Flat came out photoreal from the same detailed prompt, so don't use it for this look.
- **Known failures**: none in three test takes (a woman waving, sipping from a mug and pointing at a laptop, against line-art frames and a window). Risks still worth watching: shading or gradients creeping onto the character, the face gaining detail as the camera moves, background line art wobbling or redrawing, and drift toward 3D.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
