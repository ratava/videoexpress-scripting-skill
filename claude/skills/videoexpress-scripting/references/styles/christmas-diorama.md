# Christmas Diorama (glossy ceramic miniature village on a fireplace mantel)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Macro photograph of a fireplace mantel miniature Christmas diorama, widescreen 16:9, eye level with the mantel shelf, shallow depth of field: a tiny glossy hand-painted ceramic Christmas village, smooth shiny glazed porcelain buildings with glowing warm windows, cotton snow, fairy lights along the mantel edge, warm firelight and soft bokeh of tree lights behind." Video anchor: "Macro miniature Christmas diorama animation: a tiny glossy hand-painted ceramic Christmas village on a fireplace mantel, smooth shiny glaze with crisp reflections, warm glowing windows, cotton snow, fairy lights and warm firelight, shallow depth of field, a real tabletop miniature filmed close up, not a cartoon."
- **Character design**: small glossy ceramic figurines (skaters, carollers, a Santa) with simple painted faces; they move like little figurines on a turntable, gliding or turning rather than walking.
- **Materials / rendering**: smooth high-gloss glaze with crisp reflections in deep red, forest green and cream with gold trim; a toy train on a circle of track; bottle-brush trees with tiny baubles; a mirror skating pond; cotton snow on the base; a dark polished wooden mantel.
- **Light**: warm golden firelight from below or behind, fairy lights along the mantel edge, glowing windows, round bokeh of tree lights in a dark room.
- **Motion**: the train running its loop, skaters circling the pond, fairy lights twinkling, firelight flickering across the glaze; a locked camera or a slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly a macro photograph of a glossy hand-painted ceramic miniature Christmas village, smooth high-gloss glaze with crisp reflections, warm golden firelight and fairy-light glow, soft round bokeh behind, shallow depth of field, stable colour and light throughout."
- **Guards**: no crackle or distressed paint, no chipped or whitewashed wood, no real people, no cartoon or illustration look, no other text, no watermark. Keep the glossy ceramic finish.
- **Summer variant**: swap the cotton snow and firelight for a sunny window, a little ceramic beach and Christmas bush (see `festive-addons.md`, Festive Summer).
- **Known failures**: none in the look in the test (a ceramic church, bakery and cottages, a red toy train, three skaters and a CHRISTMAS DIORAMA sign, 2 takes): the gloss, bokeh and firelight held, and the sign stayed sharp. VE pushed in further than "eases in very slowly"; name the end framing.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
