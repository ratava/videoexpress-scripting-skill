# Embroidery on Linen (cotton floss, visible stitches)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Hand embroidery in cotton floss on natural oatmeal linen, photographed flat and straight on, widescreen 16:9, the linen filling the whole frame with its weave clearly visible." Video anchor: "Hand-embroidery stop-motion animation: cotton floss satin stitch, French knots and back-stitch outlines on natural linen, every stitch visible, shapes that move in whole-stitch steps with a gentle stepped cadence, stitched, not rendered."
- **Character design**: dense satin stitch following feather or fur direction, French-knot eyes and flower centres, back-stitched outlines in a darker thread, stem stitch for branches, long-and-short stitch for leaves. Name every element's stitch.
- **Materials / rendering**: thread sheen catching light, individual stitches distinct, slight puckering around dense satin stitch, linen weave visible between shapes, a few loose thread ends. Only thread and linen.
- **Light**: soft even light from one side, read as sheen on stitches and a faint stitch shadow.
- **Motion**: head tilt, wings opening a little, in whole-stitch steps; everything else still; camera locked.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly cotton-floss embroidery on natural linen, individual stitches distinct throughout, stepped whole-stitch cadence, stitches that never stretch, flow or blur, linen weave visible between every shape, consistent stitching of the subject."
- **Guards**: no painted or printed areas, no smooth digital colour, no gradients, no photographic subject, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no live-action look, no stitches changing length, no hoop in shot unless asked.
- **Known failures**: none in the test take (bluebird on a flowering branch). The sibling preset is `wool.md`; embroidery is its flat cousin.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
