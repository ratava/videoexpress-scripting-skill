# Chalkboard (chalk on a dark green board)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Chalk drawing on a dark green school blackboard, widescreen 16:9, the board filling the whole frame edge to edge." Video anchor: "Chalk-on-blackboard animation: white and pastel chalk strokes on a dark green school board, dusty hand-drawn lines that re-draw frame to frame in a gentle stepped cadence, drawn in chalk, not rendered."
- **Character design**: simple diagram-like drawings in white chalk outline with pastel chalk fills (pale yellow, blue, pink); dotted-dash paths and arcs are the medium's signature.
- **Materials / rendering**: broken dusty strokes, thicker where pressed harder, chalk-dust haze around strokes, faint ghost smudges of erased drawings, slight board sheen and scratches, a thin wooden frame at the edges. Only chalk colours on dark green.
- **Light**: even flat classroom light.
- **Motion**: an object travelling along a dotted path in stepped hops, scribbled chalk smoke; camera locked on the whole board.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly chalk on a dark green blackboard, dusty broken chalk lines, stepped hand-drawn cadence, chalk dust haze visible throughout, the board and its fixed drawings holding still, consistent drawing of the subject."
- **Guards**: no photographic or 3D subject, no glossy rendering, no gradients, no paint, no realistic fire or smoke, no live-action look, no new drawings appearing, no erasing of fixed elements, no equations.
- **Known failures**: none in the test take (chalk rocket along a dotted arc to a planet).
