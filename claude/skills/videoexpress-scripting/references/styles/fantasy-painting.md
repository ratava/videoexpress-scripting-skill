# Fantasy Painting (epic fantasy book-cover art, painterly digital painting with glowing light)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Epic fantasy digital painting in the style of a fantasy book cover, rich painterly brushwork, glowing magical light, deep atmospheric colour, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Epic fantasy digital painting come to life: rich painterly brushwork with visible texture, glowing magical light and floating motes, deep atmospheric blues and golds, ancient monumental settings, soft painted edges in the distance and crisp detail on the hero, slow majestic motion, painted, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: heroic, idealised figures with painted rather than rendered faces; flowing garments (robes, cloaks, banners) that carry movement; silhouettes framed by light; identity carried by costume colour, hair and a signature prop (a staff, an orb, a sword).
- **Materials / rendering**: painted with visible brush texture and soft blending, like a digital oil painting; fine detail where the light falls (embroidery, moss, carved stone) and loose painted masses in shadow and distance; glowing light sources (orbs, runes, lanterns) with soft bloom and painted rays; layered atmosphere (mist, waterfalls, motes).
- **Light**: magical and dramatic: a strong glowing source on or near the hero against a twilight or moonlit scene, warm-cool contrast (gold against deep blue), light spilling across mossy stone.
- **Motion**: slow, majestic beats (an orb brightening, robes and hair stirring, motes drifting upward, a hand raised), one per segment; locked camera or a slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly epic fantasy digital painting, rich painterly brushwork with visible texture, glowing magical light, deep atmospheric colour, the hero keeps her design and proportions, painted detail stays painted, stable colour and light throughout, no conversion to 3D CG or photography."
- **Guards**: no 3D CG render look, no game-engine shine, no photorealism, no live-action look, no anime styling, no flat cartoon colour, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Mystic Fantasy.
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a young mage raising a glowing orb at a forest shrine at twilight, a FANTASY PAINTING banner): the painterly brushwork and glow held, with no drift to 3D render. Risks still worth watching: drift toward 3D game-render shine (the neighbour of Game Cinematic).
