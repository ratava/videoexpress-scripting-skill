# Voxel (a world built from cubes, blocky game look)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Voxel art 3D render, a whole world built from uniform cubes, blocky characters and animals made of cube shapes, pixel-textured block faces, warm cinematic lighting, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Voxel animation: everything built from uniform cubes with pixel-textured faces, blocky cube-headed characters and square animals, square trees, square clouds and cube water, characters moving in stiff, blocky steps, a cinematic voxel world, not smooth 3D and not photographed."
- **Character design**: cube head, rectangular body and limbs with no elbows or knees; faces as a few flat pixels (eyes, mouth); clothes as flat pixel textures; animals as stacked boxes with pixel faces. Name the creatures generically (a blocky pig, a blocky sheep); never name a game or its characters.
- **Materials / rendering**: every surface is made of axis-aligned cubes with low-resolution pixel textures (grass tops, dirt sides, stone, wooden planks, leaf blocks); water and lava as flat cube layers; light with soft shadows and sometimes volumetric sun rays for a cinematic look.
- **Light**: warm sunset or bright daylight, soft shadows, optional god rays through square clouds.
- **Motion**: stiff, swinging blocky limb movement, a hop, a head turn, a pig waddling, water flowing in block steps; locked camera or a slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly voxel art, everything built from uniform cubes with pixel textures, blocky characters with cube heads and no rounded shapes, square trees and clouds, shapes stay blocky and grid-aligned, stable colour and light throughout."
- **Guards**: no rounded or smooth shapes, no realistic characters, no photorealism, no 2D flat drawing, no game logos or game names, no interface or hotbar, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Minecraft Style (also Minecraft Cinematic Style). In prompts, describe "voxel" and "blocky"; don't use the game's name.
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a blocky adventurer waving by a cabin at sunset, with a pig, a sheep, a waterfall and a VOXEL WORLD sign): everything stayed cube-built and the sign held. Risks still worth watching: characters gaining rounded shapes or elbows, and blocks softening as they move.
