# Game Cinematic (high-end video-game trailer CG, heroic and atmospheric)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "High-end video game cinematic trailer render, detailed real-time 3D characters and environments, dramatic golden-hour lighting with volumetric haze, heroic composition, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "High-end video game cinematic trailer animation: detailed real-time 3D characters with game-art proportions, richly detailed armour, leather and cloth materials, epic environments with volumetric light shafts, drifting mist and particles, dramatic rim light, smooth heroic motion, a game trailer, not live action."
- **Character design**: heroic, slightly idealised game-art proportions; detailed costumes (layered leather, armour plates, straps, cloaks, weapons); faces sculpted and clean, with readable expressions but without real-skin pores; silhouettes strong enough to read at a distance.
- **Materials / rendering**: physically based materials (worn leather, scratched metal, wool cloaks, mossy stone) with crisp detail; strong depth: foreground rocks, mid-ground hero, misty far background; atmospheric perspective; floating particles (dust, embers, pollen) in the light.
- **Light**: dramatic and directional: golden-hour or moonlit key, strong rim light on the hero, god rays through mist, warm-cool contrast.
- **Motion**: weighty, purposeful hero beats (a cloak billowing, drawing a bow, a turn of the head, a stride to a ledge), particles drifting; slow cinematic camera (a slow push or a gentle arc), one move per segment.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly high-end video game cinematic CG, detailed real-time 3D characters and environments, physically based materials, volumetric light and mist, the hero keeps her design and proportions, stable lighting and colour throughout, no conversion to live action or 2D."
- **Guards**: no live-action look, no real-skin pores or photographic grain, no 2D drawing, no cartoon proportions, no anime styling, no HUD or game interface, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: AAA Game Style (it garbles carved lettering; put titles on a plaque or banner with raised letters instead).
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a hooded ranger on a cliff drawing her bow at dawn, a GAME CINEMATIC bronze plaque): the game-render look, rim light and materials held, with no drift to live action. Risks still worth watching: drift toward photoreal live action and the face losing its game-art sculpting.
