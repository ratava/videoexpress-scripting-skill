# Chibi (cute super-deformed 3D characters, soft vinyl-toy look)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Cute chibi 3D animated illustration, super-deformed characters with very large heads and tiny bodies, soft rounded vinyl-toy shapes, warm cosy lighting, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Cute chibi 3D animation: super-deformed characters with very large heads, huge sparkling eyes and tiny rounded bodies, soft smooth vinyl-toy surfaces, rounded chunky props, warm glowing light, gentle bouncy motion, a cosy miniature world."
- **Character design**: head about as tall as the whole body below it; huge glossy eyes with two highlights, tiny nose, small mouth, soft blush; short stubby limbs and mitten-like hands; hair and hats as big smooth chunky shapes; clothes simplified to a few rounded pieces. Animals equally chibi: round, big-eyed, small-limbed.
- **Materials / rendering**: smooth soft surfaces like painted vinyl toys or clay without fingerprints; rounded, slightly oversized props (a chunky cauldron, fat bottles, a big wooden spoon); soft subsurface glow on skin; a miniature diorama feel with soft background blur.
- **Light**: warm, cosy key light (candles, lanterns, sunset), glowing accents (potions, fairy lights), soft rim light separating the character from the background.
- **Motion**: one small, bouncy beat per segment (a stir, a hop, a blink, a head tilt), with a little squash and settle; locked camera or one gentle push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly cute chibi 3D animation, super-deformed proportions with a very large head and tiny body, huge sparkling eyes, smooth vinyl-toy surfaces, rounded chunky props, characters keep their proportions and designs, stable colour and warm glow throughout."
- **Guards**: no realistic proportions, no adult body shapes, no photorealism, no live-action look, no realistic skin pores or fur, no 2D flat drawing, no harsh shadows, no text other than the named sign, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Chibi Style (renders in this 3D chibi look, not 2D).
- **Known failures**: none in three test takes (a tiny witch stirring a cauldron, a big bubble popping, a hop and a giggle, a sleepy cat opening one eye, with a CHIBI sign held from the start frame). Risks still worth watching: proportions growing toward normal as the character moves, the eyes shrinking, and small props (the spoon, bottles) changing shape.
