# Marker Doodle (black felt-tip line art with flat marker colour, whiteboard explainer)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Hand-drawn marker doodle illustration, black felt-tip pen line art with flat marker colour fills on plain white paper, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Hand-drawn marker doodle animation: confident black felt-tip outlines of even weight, flat marker colour fills with a few visible streaks, plain white paper background with lots of empty space, simple loose sketch details, drawn by hand, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: a simple cartoon person drawn in one confident black outline; a round face with two small dot or oval eyes, a short curved smile, small rosy cheek dots; hair as a solid black marker shape; clothes as flat marker colour inside the outline, with a few pen lines for folds and cuffs; simple hands with drawn fingers.
- **Materials / rendering**: black felt-tip outlines on everything, slightly uneven like real pen; flat marker fills in a few bright colours (mustard yellow, sky blue, leaf green, terracotta), with faint streaks and the white of the paper showing at the edges; background objects (windows, skyline, furniture) as quick line drawings with little or no fill. Plain white paper, no texture beyond the marker streaks.
- **Light**: none modelled; flat and even. No shadows except a short pen hatch under a foot or object at most.
- **Motion**: one simple gesture per segment (a wave, lifting a mug, pointing), then a hold; locked camera; lines stay drawn, not redrawn each frame.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly hand-drawn marker doodle, black felt-tip outlines of even weight, flat marker colour fills, plain white paper, lines stay put and do not wobble, boil or redraw, stable colours and character drawing throughout."
- **Guards**: no gradients, no soft shading, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no realistic skin, no watercolour washes, no paint texture, no digital vector smoothness, no text, letters or numbers, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Doodle Style Illustration.
- **Known failures**: none in three test takes (a woman waving, sipping from a mug and pointing at a desk): outlines and marker fills stayed stable, with no boiling or shading. A figure moving across busy line art once picked up a white halo outline. Risks still worth watching: lines boiling or redrawing, and the fills turning into smooth vector or soft shading.
