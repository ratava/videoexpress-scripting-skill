# Modern 2D Cartoon (contemporary TV and streaming animation, clean line and soft cel shading)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Modern 2D cartoon illustration in the style of a contemporary animated TV series, clean confident dark linework, bright cel colours with soft two-tone shading, a softly painted background, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Modern 2D cartoon animation in the style of a contemporary animated TV series: clean dark outlines of steady weight, bright cel colours with soft two-tone shading, expressive cartoon faces, a softly painted storybook background, smooth character animation, drawn and painted, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: appealing cartoon proportions (slightly large heads, big expressive eyes with a highlight, small noses, readable mouths); simple confident shapes; hair as big clean masses (curls, puffs, fringes) with a few inner lines; clothes as flat colour with simple folds and one shade tone. Animals cartoonish and expressive, with fur as a few clean tufts.
- **Materials / rendering**: clean dark-brown or black outlines on characters and props; bright saturated cel fills with one soft shade and an occasional highlight; backgrounds softly painted with gentle gradients and less line, so characters pop; simple readable props (a bicycle, an awning, shop windows).
- **Light**: bright sunny daylight with soft cel shadows on one side of each figure and short soft cast shadows on the ground; a warm, cheerful palette.
- **Motion**: one clear, slightly exaggerated beat per segment (a lick of an ice cream, a dog's head tilt and tail wag, a laugh), with anticipation and a settle; locked camera or one gentle push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly modern 2D cartoon animation, clean dark outlines of steady weight, bright cel colours with soft two-tone shading, softly painted background, characters keep their proportions and designs, lines do not wobble or boil, no conversion to 3D or photography, stable colour throughout."
- **Guards**: no 3D rendering, no CGI, no photorealism, no realistic skin or fur, no live-action look, no anime styling, no rubber-hose 1930s look, no paper or canvas texture, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Detailed Cartoon Style.
- **Known failures**: none in three test takes (a girl licking an ice cream and giggling at her dog outside a shop with a MODERN CARTOON sign): clean outlines and cel shading throughout, no drift to 3D, sign steady. Risks still worth watching: drift toward 3D CGI and the dog's fur turning realistic.
