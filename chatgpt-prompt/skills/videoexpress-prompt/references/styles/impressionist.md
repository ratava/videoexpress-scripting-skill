# Impressionist (broken-colour oil painting, light and atmosphere over detail)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Impressionist oil painting en plein air, short broken brushstrokes and dabs of pure unblended colour, shimmering light and atmosphere, soft edges, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Impressionist oil painting animation: short broken brushstrokes and dabs of pure unblended colour laid side by side, shimmering dappled light, soft dissolving edges, visible canvas and paint texture, colour mixing in the eye, a painting come gently to life, painted, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: figures suggested rather than detailed: a face as a few warm strokes with no fine features, clothes as patches of light and shadow colour, hands and feet as small dabs; identity carried by silhouette, colour and props (a hat, a parasol), not by facial detail.
- **Materials / rendering**: short comma-like strokes and dabs of pure colour placed side by side, never blended smooth; shadows in blues and violets, not black or grey; light in warm yellows, pinks and creams; water as horizontal flecks of reflected colour; foliage as clusters of dabs; visible canvas weave in thin areas; edges soft and dissolving into the surroundings.
- **Light**: the subject of the painting: bright outdoor light (morning, noon or evening), dappled sun through leaves, coloured shadows, sparkle on water.
- **Motion**: gentle, atmospheric motion (water rippling, leaves stirring, a parasol turning, a slow turn of the head), with the brushstrokes shimmering slightly as light moves; slow, calm cadence; locked camera or a very slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly impressionist oil painting, short broken brushstrokes and dabs of pure unblended colour, coloured shadows, soft dissolving edges, canvas texture, strokes shimmer gently with the light but the composition and figures stay in place, no conversion to photography, smooth digital painting or 3D, stable palette throughout."
- **Guards**: no photorealism, no smooth blended digital painting, no sharp detailed faces, no hard outlines, no black shadows, no 3D rendering, no live-action look, no other text, no watermark. Don't name painters in prompts; describe the technique.
- **Artistly match**: none close. Artistly's Wall Art Style gave a smooth, semi-realistic garden painting, not broken colour.
- **Known failures**: none in the test takes (a woman with a parasol on a footbridge over a lily pond, an IMPRESSIONIST signboard): the broken-colour brushwork held without smoothing or boiling. Risks still worth watching: the brushwork smoothing into a digital painting, and strokes boiling.
