# Anime: Shōjo (fine line, luminous eyes, screentone, petals)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "A shōjo-style Japanese animation still, widescreen 16:9: very fine delicate dark-brown ink lines of varying thinness, large luminous eyes with layered irises and starburst highlights, slender elongated figures, soft cel shading with gentle gradient edges, grey screentone dot textures on the shadows of the clothing, a soft palette of pink, lavender, cream and pale gold, and sparkle and petal motifs floating in the air." Video anchor: "Shōjo-style Japanese animation: very fine delicate ink lines, large luminous eyes with starburst highlights, soft gradient-edged cel shading, screentone dot textures, a soft pink and lavender palette, with petals and sparkle motifs drifting through the air, drawn, not rendered in 3D."
- **Character design**: long wavy hair with fine individual strands, very large eyes with starburst highlights and long lashes, small nose, lace collars and ribbons, slender build.
- **Materials / rendering**: screentone dots in clothing shadows and wallpaper; four-point sparkle motifs; soft radiating window light; floating petals.
- **Light**: soft diffused window light, warm fill, a faint pink glow around the hair.
- **Motion**: curtains lifting, petals drifting, hair stirring in fine strands, eyes closing and opening, a sparkle glinting in each eye; one very slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly shōjo-style hand-drawn animation: fine delicate lines, soft gradient-edged cel shading, visible screentone dots, starburst eye highlights, soft pink and lavender palette, stable line art and colour throughout, consistent character drawing."
- **Guards**: no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no thick bold outlines, no hard high-contrast shading, no live-action look.
- **Known failures**: none in the test take (girl at a blossom window). Title lettering tested as SHOJO ANIME (no macron) and rendered cleanly.
