# Snow Globe (a tiny scene inside a glass globe, snow swirling)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Close-up product photograph of a glass snow globe, widescreen 16:9, the globe large in the centre of the frame and fully inside it: a tiny scene inside the clear glass dome, white snowflakes suspended in clear water, curved glass with crisp highlights and soft reflections, a slight magnifying distortion near the rim, a round polished wooden base." Video anchor: "Snow globe close-up animation: a real glass snow globe photographed close up, clear water and curved glass with crisp highlights, a tiny scene inside, white snow swirling through the water, realistic, not a cartoon."
- **Character design**: tiny static figures inside the globe (a snowman, a little house, a tree, a skater); they don't move, the snow does.
- **Materials / rendering**: realistic glass and water; crisp specular highlights; room reflections on the curve; a polished wooden base with a gold plaque (good for a title); snow as small white flakes.
- **Light**: warm glowing windows inside the globe; soft blue night or warm room light outside, with bokeh.
- **Motion**: snow swirling in slow spirals and settling, tiny lights twinkling, chimney smoke swaying inside the glass; the globe itself stays still.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly a close-up photograph of a glass snow globe, realistic curved glass and water with crisp highlights and soft room reflections, the globe keeps its shape, stable colour and light throughout."
- **Guards**: no shaking or moving the globe, no hands, no cracks in the glass, no snow outside the globe, no cartoon or illustration look, no other text, no watermark.
- **Summer variant**: a beach or tropical scene inside, with gold glitter instead of snow (see `festive-addons.md`, Festive Summer).
- **Known failures**: none in the look in the test (a log cabin, a decorated tree and a snowman inside, a SNOW GLOBE plaque, 2 takes): glass, water, swirl and glow held. Both takes pushed in and tilted up despite "locked", so a plaque on the base left the frame after about 4 s; put a title near the top of the globe or accept a short hold.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
