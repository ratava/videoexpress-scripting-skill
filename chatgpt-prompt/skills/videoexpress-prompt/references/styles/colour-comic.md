# Colour Comic (bright ligne-claire adventure comic, clean even ink and flat colour)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Bright colour adventure comic art in the clean-line tradition, crisp even-weight black ink outlines, flat bright colour fills with little or no shading, detailed clear backgrounds, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Bright colour adventure comic animation in the clean-line tradition: crisp black ink outlines of even weight on everything, flat bright colour fills with little or no shading, clear readable shapes, detailed backgrounds drawn with the same clean line, printed-comic look, drawn and inked, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: realistic proportions with simplified, slightly caricatured faces (dot or small oval eyes, simple noses, clear expressions); clothes as clean shapes with a few fold lines; characters and backgrounds drawn with the same even line, no line-weight variation and no hatching.
- **Materials / rendering**: uniform black ink line, flat saturated colour (sunny yellows, sky blues, brick reds, earth oranges), at most one flat shadow tone; skies as flat blue with a few outlined clouds; vehicles and machinery drawn precisely with clean ellipses and panel lines; slight printed-paper warmth.
- **Light**: bright, clear daylight; shadows as small flat shapes, never soft gradients or heavy black.
- **Motion**: clear, readable gestures (a turn, a wipe of the brow, a thumbs-up, a dog's tail wag), one beat per segment; locked camera or one gentle push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly bright colour comic art in the clean-line style, crisp even-weight black ink outlines, flat bright colour with little or no shading, backgrounds in the same clean line, lines stay crisp and do not wobble or boil, no conversion to 3D or photography, stable colour throughout."
- **Guards**: no noir shadows, no heavy black areas, no hatching or cross-hatching, no halftone dots, no gradients, no painterly texture, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no anime styling, no speech bubbles, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Comicbook Illustration.
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a mechanic fixing a yellow 1950s car outside a desert garage, a puff of steam, a thumbs-up, a COLOUR COMIC sign). Risks still worth watching: drift toward the noir Comic Book preset (heavy blacks), lines thickening or varying, and colours picking up gradients.
