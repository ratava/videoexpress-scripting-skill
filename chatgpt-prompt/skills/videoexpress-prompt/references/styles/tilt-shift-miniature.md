# Tilt-Shift Miniature (real scenes that look like tiny scale models)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Tilt-shift miniature photograph, a real scene seen from high above that looks like a tiny scale model, a narrow band of sharp focus across the middle with strong blur at the top and bottom, bright saturated toy-like colours, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Tilt-shift miniature time-lapse: a real scene from a high angle that looks like a tiny toy model, a narrow horizontal band of sharp focus with strong blur above and below, bright saturated toy-like colours and hard sunlight, tiny people and vehicles moving in quick, slightly jerky steps."
- **Character design**: people and vehicles tiny and numerous, seen from above; individual faces never visible; crowds read as dots of bright clothing.
- **Materials / rendering**: everything looks like painted plastic and model-railway scenery: punchy saturation, slightly raised contrast, crisp detail only in the focus band; strong lens blur (not fog) at the top and bottom of the frame; a high camera angle of about 30 to 50 degrees, never eye level.
- **Light**: hard, bright midday or afternoon sun with crisp small shadows, as on a model under a lamp.
- **Motion**: many small movements at once, sped up like a time-lapse: cars and people shuffling along, a Ferris wheel turning, waves rolling; quick, slightly stepped cadence; locked high camera.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly tilt-shift miniature look, a narrow band of sharp focus with strong blur at the top and bottom, high camera angle, bright saturated toy-like colours, tiny people and vehicles, the focus band stays in place, stable colour and light throughout."
- **Guards**: no eye-level view, no close-ups of faces, no fog or haze in place of lens blur, no muted colours, no illustration or 3D cartoon look, no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Miniature.
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a seaside fairground with a Ferris wheel, a carousel and a MINIATURE rooftop billboard, seen from high above): the focus band, lens blur and toy-like colour held. Risks still worth watching: the blur disappearing, and the scene reading as an ordinary aerial photo.
