# Crayon Drawing (a young child's wax-crayon drawing come to life)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "A young child's wax crayon drawing on plain white paper, naive hand-drawn picture with wobbly outlines and scribbled crayon colouring, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "A young child's wax crayon drawing come to life: wobbly hand-drawn crayon outlines, scribbled waxy colour fills with visible strokes and white paper showing through, simple naive shapes, plain white paper background, gentle bobbing hand-animated motion, drawn with crayons, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: how a five-to-seven-year-old draws: a round head with dot eyes and a big curved smile, stick-thin arms and legs, triangle dresses, hair as a few scribbled strokes; animals as simple ovals with dot spots and stick legs; a sun with a smiling face and straight rays; a square house with a triangle roof.
- **Materials / rendering**: waxy crayon strokes in bright primary colours, coloured in loosely with back-and-forth scribbles that go over the lines, uneven pressure, white paper visible between strokes; a green crayon strip of grass along the bottom; puffy clouds as scribbled blue outlines; no blending, no shading, no perspective.
- **Light**: none; flat, even, as on a sheet of paper. The sun is a drawing, not a light source.
- **Motion**: simple bobbing motion, as if a child's drawing were puppeted: a kite bobs on its string, a dog hops, a sun's rays wiggle, a figure waves; one beat per segment; locked camera.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly a young child's wax crayon drawing on white paper, wobbly crayon outlines, scribbled waxy colour with white paper showing through, naive shapes, no shading, no perspective, shapes keep their simple designs, strokes stay put, stable colours throughout."
- **Guards**: no realistic proportions, no shading or gradients, no perspective depth, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no polished illustration, no smooth digital fills, no paper photograph (no desk, no hands, no shadows), no other text, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: Children Drawing Style.
- **Known failures**: none in six test takes (a girl flying a kite with her dog, a smiling sun and a house): the crayon look and the CRAYON DRAWING lettering held throughout. Risks still worth watching: the drawing turning into a polished storybook illustration, scribbles smoothing into flat digital fills, and the lettering garbling as the scene moves.
