# Festive add-ons (Christmas winter or summer, for any style)

**Type**: add-on, tested in production (pass)

Seasonal blocks to add to any style's prompts, not styles of their own. Use the style's own sheet as usual, then add the block's image line to the start-image prompt (after the scene) and its video line to the video anchor, and restate the style's stability line. Half the world has Christmas in summer: ask which one the video needs, and don't assume snow.

## Festive Winter

- **Image line**: "Festive winter: soft snow falling steadily, a fresh layer of snow on roofs, branches and the ground, glittering frost along the window panes, long clear icicles hanging from every roofline, strings of warm fairy lights, a decorated Christmas tree with a star, cosy glowing windows against a cool blue dusk sky."
- **Video line**: "Festive winter: soft snow falling steadily, snow on the roofs and ground, icicles on the rooflines, warm twinkling fairy lights and a decorated Christmas tree, cosy glowing windows against a cool blue dusk."
- **Motion**: snow falling steadily, fairy lights twinkling, the tree star glinting.
- **Stability**: "the falling snow stays light so the scene stays clearly visible".
- **Guards**: no blizzard or white-out.
- **Tested on**: Gouache Picture Book (children building a snowman in a village square at dusk, 2 takes). The gouache look held with steady light snow and twinkling lights; icicles came out faint, so name them in the scene if they matter.

## Festive Summer

- **Image line**: "Festive summer: bright midday summer sun high in a clear blue sky, a decorated Christmas tree with red and gold baubles and silver tinsel glinting in the sunshine, a red-flowering Christmas bush, tinsel wrapped around a beach umbrella, red Santa hats worn with swimwear and shorts, a backyard or beach barbecue with a little heat shimmer, warm golden light with crisp short shadows, no snow anywhere."
- **Video line**: "Festive summer: bright midday sun, clear blue sky, a decorated Christmas tree with tinsel and baubles glinting, red Christmas bush, Santa hats with summer clothes, a barbecue, no snow."
- **Motion**: tinsel fluttering and glinting in a warm breeze, baubles sparkling, barbecue smoke and heat shimmer, waves or a sprinkler, Santa-hat pom-poms bouncing.
- **Guards**: no snow, no winter clothing, no frost, no snowflakes, no falling sparkles. VE links Christmas with falling snow: in the action, say the air is dry and sunny with nothing falling from the sky.
- **Local touches**: Australian (Christmas bush, beach cricket, prawns on the barbecue, a lifesaver flag), New Zealand (red-flowering pōhutukawa), South African (a braai), South American (a summer garden party). Use the one that fits the audience.
- **Tested on**: Cinematic Photoreal (an Australian beach on Christmas Day: a tinsel tree in the sand, a tinsel-wrapped umbrella, Christmas bush, a family in Santa hats at a barbecue, 2 takes). The summer-Christmas look held with no snow; a "gentle drift" ran wide, so name the end framing.

## Tested across style families

Each add-on was tested on a spread of styles (2 takes each); the blocks above went in unchanged unless noted.

| Style | Add-on | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Gouache Picture Book | Winter | Pass: flat matte gouache held; icicles faint |
| Modern 2D Cartoon | Winter | Pass: clean line and cel colour held, snow stayed light |
| Claymation | Winter | Pass: plasticine texture held, stepped snow |
| Watercolor | Winter | Pass: washes and ink held, icicles clear |
| Voxel | Winter | Pass: cube world held; snow as tiny cubes, icicles as stacked thin blocks (say so) |
| Cinematic Photoreal | Summer | Pass: no snow |
| Chibi | Summer | Look held, but falling sparkles or flakes appeared late in 2 of 2 takes despite "no snow"; use the dry-air line and the extra guards above |
| Wool | Summer | Pass: felt and wool fibres held |
| Linocut | Summer | Failed as written (full colour, blue sky and green tree); passed once the block was rewritten in the palette's terms |
| Stickman | Summer | Failed as written ("swimwear and shorts" gave cartoon bodies); passed with the stickman master in Reference Photo 1 and a Santa hat as the only clothing |

**Rules from the tests:**
- **Limited-palette styles** (Linocut, Risograph, Engraving, Greyscale Airbrush, Vintage Horror, Blueprint, Chalkboard, Charcoal and Chalk): the block's colour words override the palette. Rewrite it in the style's own terms, e.g. for a black-and-red linocut: "festive summer, told only in black and red ink: a big round sun as a ring of carved rays, tinsel as carved zigzag lines, baubles as round dots, red Santa hats, the sky left as bare paper, no other colours".
- **Styles defined by their characters** (Stickman, Chibi, Voxel): keep the festive clothing to a single item (a Santa hat) and use the style's own reference where it has one; clothing words pull characters away from the style.
- Material styles (Claymation, Wool, Gingerbread, Holiday Stop-Motion) take the blocks as they are, with the snow or tinsel made of the style's material.
