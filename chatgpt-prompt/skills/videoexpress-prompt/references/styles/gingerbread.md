# Gingerbread (a world baked from gingerbread, icing and sweets)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Macro food photograph of a gingerbread Christmas village, widescreen 16:9, low three-quarter view, shallow depth of field: houses of baked brown gingerbread panels with crisp edges, white royal icing piped along every roofline as dripping icicles, gumdrop and chocolate-button roofs, clear yellow boiled-sugar windows glowing warm, candy-cane lampposts, gingerbread men with piped icing faces, powdered-sugar snow." Video anchor: "Gingerbread village macro animation: a real gingerbread Christmas village photographed close up, baked brown gingerbread with crumbs and sugar crystals, white piped royal icing, gumdrop roofs, candy canes and powdered-sugar snow, warm cosy light, real food come to life, not a cartoon."
- **Character design**: gingerbread men and women as flat cookie shapes with piped icing smiles, buttons and cuffs; they move stiffly, like cookies come to life.
- **Materials / rendering**: real baked texture with crumbs and sugar crystals; crisp white piped icing; glossy gumdrops; peppermint swirls; pretzel posts; powdered-sugar snow. A large cookie sign with piped lettering holds well.
- **Light**: warm cosy kitchen light, glowing sugar-glass windows, golden bokeh of fairy lights.
- **Motion**: a gingerbread man waddling or waving, powdered sugar drifting down, windows glowing; a locked camera or a slow push.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly a macro food photograph of a gingerbread village, real baked gingerbread texture with crumbs and sugar crystals, crisp white piped icing, glossy gumdrops, warm golden light and soft bokeh, gingerbread men stay flat cookie shapes, stable colour and light throughout."
- **Guards**: no real people, no cartoon or illustration look, no melting, no crumbling buildings, no other text, no watermark.
- **Known failures**: none in the look in the test (a gingerbread street with gumdrop roofs, candy-cane lamps, gingerbread men and a GINGERBREAD cookie sign, 2 takes): texture and icing held, and the sign stayed sharp as it was enlarged. VE pushed in further than "eases in very slowly".
