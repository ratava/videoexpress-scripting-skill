# Anime: Shōnen Cyberpunk (shōnen line and action, cybernetic character design)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Shōnen action-style Japanese anime still, widescreen 16:9: thick black ink outlines, hard two-tone cel shading with deep black shadows, flat colours with natural warm skin tones." Keep image prompts short: the defining features of a detailed character go straight after this line. Video anchor: "Shōnen action-style Japanese animation with cyberpunk character design: thick dynamic black ink outlines, hard high-contrast two-tone cel shading with deep black shadow shapes, cybernetic parts drawn in the same ink line and flat cel colour as skin and cloth, a limited cool palette of blacks, greys, gunmetal and slate teal with natural warm skin tones and one orange accent, speed lines and a single impact frame, hand-drawn action timing with a fast beat followed by a long hold, flat two-dimensional animation drawn and painted by hand."
- **Character design**: Shōnen proportions and line (thick, variable outlines, hard black shadow shapes) with cybernetic parts built into the body: riveted face plates, a single glowing machine lens, mechanical limbs. One hot accent colour for the whole cast, carried by one feature (a machine eye). Describe asymmetric cybernetics per body side and per image side. Simple, familiar weapons drift least.
- **Materials / rendering**: metal drawn with the same ink line and flat cel colour as skin and cloth, with a single hard highlight; flat colour fills; backgrounds simplified toward graphic shapes.
- **Light**: a hard key from one high corner, hard-edged two-tone cel shadows, the face kept clearly lit.
- **Motion**: the Shōnen cadence: slow tension, one fast beat lasting well under a second with radiating speed lines and a single stark white-and-black impact frame, then a long motionless hold. One strike per segment. Camera locked for weapon action.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly shōnen action hand-drawn animation: thick black outlines, hard two-tone cel shading with deep black shadow shapes, metal drawn as flat cel colour with ink line, limited cool palette with the single [accent] accent in [the feature that carries it], stable line art and colour, consistent character drawing."
- **Guards** (in [NEGATIVES]): no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no glossy chrome or reflective rendered metal, no soft gradient shading, no thin delicate lines, no pastel colours, no hot-pink or magenta neon.
- **Known failures**: a cool-grey palette clause in an image prompt turned warm or pale skin grey in every take where it appeared; use "flat colours with natural warm skin tones" in image prompts. Long image prompts dropped a character's detailed cybernetic features; build detailed characters in stages (bible.md, Detailed character reference sheets). Without "drawn as flat cel colour with ink line", metal rendered as glossy 3D chrome.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
