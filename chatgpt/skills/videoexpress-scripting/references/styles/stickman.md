# Stickman (2D stickman story cartoon)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "2D stickman cartoon illustration, crisp stable black ink outlines, flat matte cel colours, simple drawn shadows, layered illustrated scenery, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Strictly a 2D stickman cartoon with crisp black ink outlines and flat cel colours throughout."
- **Character design**: white circular head with expressive drawn black eyes, eyebrows and mouth; one or two simple outlined costume pieces in flat colour (a scarf, a t-shirt, a hat) for identity; exactly two separate thin black arms with small black hands, exactly two thin black legs, two separate small shoes. Every person in frame, background people included, is a stickman in the same style; give recurring stickmen distinct costume colours.
- **Materials / rendering**: flat matte cel colour, no gradients beyond a soft drawn shadow under the feet; clean illustrated scenery with depth from overlap, scale and paler distant hills; animals and props simply drawn with the same outline.
- **Light**: bright, even daylight as a rule; time of day shown by sky colour, not by modelled light.
- **Motion**: readable everyday action, one beat per clip (walking, sitting, waving, picking something up); limbs swing simply; locked camera or a gentle push from one side.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly a 2D stickman cartoon, crisp black ink outlines, flat matte cel colours, simple drawn shadows, the stickman keeps his white round head, two thin arms and two thin legs, landmarks stay in place, stable colours throughout."
- **Guards**: no realistic or conventionally drawn people, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no thick or rounded limbs, no extra arms or legs, no other text, no watermark.
- **Settings**: tested with Image Type 2D and Creative mode off (not the custom-style Creative mode route the other sheets use), Automatically enhance my image prompt off, and a character master plus an empty-location reference in the Consistent Character slots. The full narrated-story format (one narrated line per clip with Narration Video) is in the VideoExpress Scripting skill, `references/narrated-story.md`.
- **Known failures**: none in the style in the test (a stickman with a red scarf walking to a park bench, then sitting with a small yellow bird, two narrated 4 s clips): outlines, flat colour, the character and the park layout all held. Risks still worth watching: counted animals or props doubling in a start image (one candidate drew two birds), and background people drawn realistic unless named as stickmen.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
