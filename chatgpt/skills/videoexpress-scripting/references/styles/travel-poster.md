# Travel Poster (vintage screen-printed travel poster, flat shapes and limited palette)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Vintage 1930s travel poster, screen-printed lithograph look, bold simplified flat shapes in a limited palette of six or seven colours, crisp edges, no outlines, a bold poster headline, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Vintage travel poster animation: bold simplified flat shapes in a limited palette, crisp hard edges with no outlines, flat colour bands for sky and sea, subtle printed paper grain, a stylised graphic landscape, gentle poster-like motion, printed, not rendered or photographed."
- **Character design**: figures as elegant simplified silhouettes built from a few flat shapes, faces with minimal or no features, long graceful proportions; clothes as single flat colours with one shadow shape.
- **Materials / rendering**: flat areas of colour with hard edges and no outlines; sky in two or three flat bands or a simple gradient band; water as flat shapes with a few stylised highlight stripes; buildings, boats and trees reduced to geometric shapes; slightly faded printed-paper grain and warm off-white paper tone; a big bold headline in clean Art Deco capitals.
- **Light**: strong sunny light shown as flat light and shadow shapes (one shadow tone per colour), long simplified shadows.
- **Motion**: gentle, poster-like motion (sailboats gliding, clouds drifting, a flag fluttering, light shimmering on the water as flat highlight stripes), one beat per segment; locked camera.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly vintage screen-printed travel poster, bold flat shapes in a limited palette, crisp hard edges, no outlines, printed paper grain, shapes stay flat and simple, the headline stays sharp, stable colours throughout, no conversion to painting, 3D or photography."
- **Guards**: no photorealism, no painterly brushwork, no gradients beyond simple sky bands, no detailed faces, no outlines, no 3D rendering, no other text than the headline, no watermark.
- **Artistly match**: none close. Artistly's Retro Vibe gave a painted retro photo, not a flat poster.
- **Known failures**: none in the test (a woman on a cliff path above a Mediterranean bay with sailboats and a lighthouse, under a TRAVEL POSTER headline): flat shapes, limited palette, paper grain and headline all held. Risks still worth watching: drift toward a painting or photo (losing the flat shapes).
