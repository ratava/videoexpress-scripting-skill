# Greyscale Airbrush (black-and-white fashion illustration, smooth tonal shading)

**Type**: custom, tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "Black-and-white airbrushed fashion illustration, a smooth greyscale digital painting on a plain off-white backdrop, widescreen 16:9." Video anchor: "Black-and-white airbrushed fashion illustration animation: smooth greyscale shading with soft airbrushed gradients, crisp painted edges on hair and garments, a full range of greys from deep black to near white, a plain off-white backdrop, painted, not photographed."
- **Character design**: illustrated but close to real proportions, slightly elongated and graceful; a smooth idealised face with clean painted features, defined lashes and brows, soft modelled cheekbones; hair as painted masses with a few crisp strands at the edges. Poses are model poses: a hand at the collar, hands on hips, a weight shift, a turn to profile.
- **Materials / rendering**: no colour anywhere; every material told by tone alone. Skin as smooth mid-to-light grey gradients with no pores; fabric by value and sheen: sequins as scattered white specks on black, florals as pale shapes on mid-grey, denim as mid-grey with lighter seams, tailoring as solid black with soft grey folds. The backdrop is an empty, even off-white with no horizon line; a faint soft floor shadow at most.
- **Light**: soft frontal studio key from slightly above, a gentle wrap of fill, no hard cast shadows; the figure's darkest garments carry the contrast against the pale backdrop.
- **Motion**: one model beat per segment (a twirl of the skirt, a hand lifted to the hair, a turn to profile and back), then a settle; smooth, unhurried cadence; locked camera, framing full length or three-quarter length.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly black-and-white airbrushed fashion illustration, smooth greyscale gradients, crisp painted edges, no colour at any point, plain off-white backdrop that stays empty, stable face and hairline, consistent character drawing throughout."
- **Guards**: no colour, no sepia tint, no photorealism, no photographic skin texture, no live-action look, no 3D rendering, no pencil or ink lines, no hatching, no film grain, no studio equipment, no set or furniture, no text or watermark.
- **Known failures**: none in five test videos. Risks still worth watching: drift to a black-and-white photograph (the look is close to photoreal), colour creeping back in, and the empty backdrop acquiring a studio set.
