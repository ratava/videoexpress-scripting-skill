# Hybrid: Claymation Puppet on a Flat Painted Backdrop

**Type**: custom hybrid (two media), tested in production (pass)

- **Medium phrase**: "A cinematic photograph of a handcrafted stop-motion set, widescreen 16:9, in two distinct layers. In front, real sculpted and photographed objects: a claymation puppet and its props, all tactile modelling clay with visible fingerprints and tool marks, lit by a real miniature studio lamp. Behind, a flat, hand-painted cardboard theatre backdrop standing upright a short way behind the puppet, painted in loose matte poster paint with visible brush strokes, slightly wrinkled, clearly a flat painted board and not a real place." Video anchor: "Handcrafted stop-motion animation: a tactile clay puppet moving in a restrained stepped stop-motion cadence under a real miniature studio lamp, in front of a flat hand-painted theatre backdrop that stays perfectly still, sculpted and photographed, not rendered."
- **Character design**: as the Claymation preset (stout forms, bead eyes, fingerprints), with props also in clay; even water as a twisted strand of clear tinted clay.
- **Materials / rendering**: the backdrop is naive matte poster paint on cardboard with a visible straight seam where it meets the painted set floor; keep that seam in every prompt, it is what makes the hybrid read.
- **Light**: one warm miniature spotlight from the front, real soft shadows from the clay figures, the backdrop lit evenly and casting no shadows of its own; shallow depth of field, puppet sharp, backdrop slightly soft.
- **Motion**: one small gesture (tipping a can, a nod), a prop that grows or straightens in two or three steps; one very slow push. Titles in this style work as cut card pinned to the backdrop and lifted away, which keeps the physical-set logic.
- **Stability line**: "Strictly tactile fingerprinted clay for the puppet and props, and flat matte poster paint on cardboard for the backdrop; stepped stop-motion cadence, the backdrop seam always visible, no sculpted depth in the backdrop, consistent puppet design."
- **Guards**: no real plants, no real sky, no glossy plastic, no 3D rendering, no smooth digital surfaces, no live-action person, no painted detail appearing on the clay, no depth or movement in the backdrop.
- **Known failures**: none in the test take (gardener puppet watering a clay sunflower). The model knows this combination from real stop-motion films, which is why it holds.

Shared image recipe, video pattern and consistency rules: `README.md` in this folder.
